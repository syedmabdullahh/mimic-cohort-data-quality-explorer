Executive Summary Report

              
MIMIC-IV Data Quality Audit — AI-Powered Clinical Analytics Platform

            

          

          
        


        

          

            
Platform Overview

            

              The MIMIC-IV Cohort & Data Quality Explorer is an explainable AI healthcare platform designed to surface 
              hidden data quality issues in electronic health records before they bias downstream AI research models.
            

            

              Using robust statistical methods (Median/MAD outlier detection, temporal consistency checks, and 
              near-duplicate identification), the system identifies and prioritizes data quality risks with 
              clinical context — enabling human reviewers to make informed decisions without altering source data.

Review Critical & High Risk Issues First

              
Prioritize flags marked as Critical or High Risk in the AI Quality Center. These represent records most likely to bias downstream models.

            

          

          

            

            

              
Address Temporal Inconsistencies

              
Temporal drift and overlapping admission windows can introduce systematic bias. Review flagged cases in the Review Workspace and document decisions.

            

          

          

            

            

              
Investigate Near-Duplicate Records

              
Near-duplicate lab events within short time windows may represent measurement errors or data entry duplicates. Accept or reject in the Review Workspace.

            

          

          

            

            

              
Export Audit Report for Governance

              
Download the PDF audit report for documentation, peer review, and regulatory compliance. The report includes all detected issues and reviewer decisions.
